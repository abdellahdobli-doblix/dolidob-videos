# Texas vs California on $75,000 — storyboard (Gemini images + Grok motion)

Figures: dolidob.com/data/take-home-2026.json (single, biweekly, no 401(k)).
Federal $7,670 · Social Security + Medicare $5,738 · California tax + SDI $4,027 · take-home TX $61,593 vs CA $57,565.

## Style block (paste at the start of EVERY Gemini image prompt, with both reference images attached)

Use the two attached characters EXACTLY as they are: same faces, hair, clothes, colors, proportions and 3D animated movie style. The boy is Dob (navy hoodie, grey t-shirt, jeans, white sneakers). The girl is Doli (mustard yellow blazer, white top, navy pants, white sneakers). Setting: a cozy modern podcast studio, dark navy walls with soft blue light, two microphones on boom arms, a small wooden table, warm lamp in the background. Vertical 9:16 frame. Keep the top 20% and the bottom 25% of the image calm and empty (background only) because titles and subtitles will be added there. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos anywhere in the image.

## Motion rules (paste at the end of EVERY Grok prompt)

Single continuous shot, no cuts, slow gentle camera push-in. Keep both characters identical to the image. No text appears. Natural, smooth, realistic body motion, nothing sudden.

### Scene 1 — Dob (≈6 s)
Line: "Doli, I got two job offers. Same pay, seventy-five thousand dollars. One in Texas, one in California."
Image: Medium shot. Dob sits at the table on the left, smiling and excited, holding up two sheets of paper, one in each hand. Doli sits on the right, turned toward him, listening with curiosity.
Grok: Dob talks excitedly to Doli and lifts the two papers one after the other, as if comparing them. Doli listens, nods once and smiles. Only Dob's mouth moves.

### Scene 2 — Doli, then Dob (≈6 s)
Lines: Doli "Nice! Same salary, but you won't take home the same amount." / Dob "Wait, really? Why not?"
Image: Medium shot. Doli on the right talks with a knowing smile, one hand raised, palm open. Dob on the left looks surprised, eyebrows up, papers lowered.
Grok: Doli speaks calmly with a small open-hand gesture. Dob's smile fades into surprise, he leans slightly forward and tilts his head as if asking "why?".

### Scene 3 — Doli (≈6 s)
Line: "Federal tax is the same in both: about seven thousand six hundred seventy dollars."
Image: Close-up of Doli, she raises one finger as she explains, confident and friendly. Dob slightly blurred at the edge of the frame, listening.
Grok: Doli explains while counting on one finger, small natural head movements. Dob in the background nods slowly.

### Scene 4 — Doli (≈6 s)
Line: "Social Security and Medicare are the same too: about fifty-seven hundred."
Image: Medium two-shot. Doli gestures with both hands as if showing two equal parts. Dob listens with arms crossed, thinking.
Grok: Doli moves her hands side by side to show "the same". Dob, arms crossed, nods slowly while thinking.

### Scene 5 — Dob, then Doli (≈8 s)
Lines: Dob "So what's different?" / Doli "The state. Texas has no income tax on wages. California takes about four thousand dollars, with state disability insurance."
Image: Medium two-shot. Doli looks at Dob seriously, one hand slightly raised. Dob holds his head with both hands, stressed and shocked.
Grok: Dob starts curious, then grabs his head with both hands in shock as Doli explains calmly. Doli keeps a sympathetic expression.

### Scene 6 — Doli (≈7 s)
Line: "Texas: about sixty-one thousand six hundred. California: about fifty-seven thousand six hundred."
Image: Medium shot. Doli holds her phone and shows the screen toward Dob (the screen is a soft glow, no readable content). Dob leans in to look, worried.
Grok: Doli turns the phone toward Dob and points at the glowing screen. Dob leans closer, eyes widening.

### Scene 7 — Dob (≈4 s)
Line: "That's four thousand dollars a year, just for the state!"
Image: Close-up of Dob, mouth open in disbelief, one hand on his forehead (facepalm). Doli at the edge of the frame, shrugging with a small smile.
Grok: Dob reacts in disbelief and slowly puts his hand on his forehead. Doli gives a small shrug and smiles.

### Scene 8 — Doli, both at the end (≈6 s)
Line: "Check your own state and salary free at dolidob dot com. Estimates only, not tax advice."
Image: Wide two-shot, both characters facing the camera, smiling. Doli points toward the viewer, Dob gives a thumbs up.
Grok: Doli speaks to the camera and points at the viewer. Dob smiles and gives a thumbs up. Both end with a friendly wave.
