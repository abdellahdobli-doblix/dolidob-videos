Same 8 scenes and the same order as set 1, so every episode script fits. Attach the two front reference images (dob-closed, doli-closed) in Gemini for every image prompt. Save the Grok clips as cafe1.mp4 ... cafe8.mp4.

## Scene 1: Dob tells Doli about his two job offers

**Gemini image:**
```
Create one vertical 9:16 image using the two attached characters EXACTLY as they are: same faces, hair, clothes, colors, proportions and 3D animated movie style. The boy is Dob (navy hoodie, grey t-shirt, jeans, white sneakers). The girl is Doli (mustard yellow blazer, white top, navy pants, white sneakers).
Setting: a cozy modern coffee shop by a large window, warm morning light, a small round wooden table with two coffee cups, plants and soft bokeh in the background.
Scene: medium shot, both sitting at the table. Dob on the left, smiling and excited, holds his phone up toward Doli as if showing her two offers. Doli on the right holds her coffee cup and listens with curiosity.
Keep the top 20% and the bottom 25% of the image as plain calm color bands. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos anywhere in the image.
```

**Grok motion:**
```
Dob talks excitedly and shows his phone to Doli, moving it a little as he explains. Doli sips her coffee, then puts the cup down and smiles with interest. Only Dob's mouth moves. Single continuous shot, no cuts, camera stays still. Keep both characters, the coffee shop and the top and bottom color bands exactly identical to the image. No text appears. Natural, smooth, realistic body motion, nothing sudden.
```
