# "Where Does My Paycheck Go?" (10-minute episode, 16:9): scene clips

The script (`paycheck-guide.json`, built by `paycheck-guide.py`) uses scenes 1-8. Each clip is looped
forward and back while its lines are spoken, so 6-10 seconds per clip is enough.
Save the finished clips as `podcast/scenes-long/scene1.mp4` ... `scene8.mp4`.

## Style block (start of EVERY image or video prompt, with the Dob and Doli reference images attached)

Use the two attached characters EXACTLY as they are: same faces, hair, clothes, colors, proportions and 3D animated movie style. The boy is Dob (navy hoodie, grey t-shirt, jeans, white sneakers). The girl is Doli (mustard yellow blazer, white top, navy pants, white sneakers). Setting: a cozy modern podcast studio, dark navy walls with soft blue light, two microphones on boom arms, a small wooden table, warm lamp in the background. Horizontal 16:9 wide frame. Keep the characters in the middle of the frame; keep the top 15% of the image, the top-right corner and the bottom 30% calm (background only), because the title, number cards and subtitles are added there. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos anywhere.

## Motion rules (end of EVERY video prompt)

Single continuous shot, no cuts, slow gentle camera movement. Keep both characters identical to the references. No text appears. Natural, smooth, subtle body motion, nothing sudden. No audio needed.

---

### Scene 1: Dob talks, excited or curious
Wide two-shot. Dob on the left talks to Doli with lively hand gestures, smiling; Doli on the right listens with interest and nods.

### Scene 2: Doli answers, Dob surprised
Medium two-shot. Doli explains with a calm open-hand gesture; Dob leans forward, eyebrows raised, curious.

### Scene 3: Doli explains (the most used clip)
Medium close-up of Doli speaking to Dob, counting on her fingers, confident and friendly. Dob slightly out of focus at the left edge, listening.

### Scene 4: Comparing two things
Medium two-shot. Doli moves both hands side by side as if comparing two amounts; Dob, arms crossed, nods slowly while thinking.

### Scene 5: Dob shocked
Medium two-shot. Dob puts both hands on his head in surprise; Doli smiles sympathetically and shrugs a little.

### Scene 6: The phone calculator
Medium two-shot. Doli holds her phone toward Dob (the screen is a soft glow, nothing readable); Dob leans in to look, interested.

### Scene 7: Dob's realization
Close-up of Dob, he laughs and taps his forehead as if something finally makes sense. Doli at the edge of the frame, smiling.

### Scene 8: Goodbye
Wide shot, both characters face the camera and smile. Doli waves, Dob gives a thumbs up. Slow pull-back.
